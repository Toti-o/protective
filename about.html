Who We Are

Royal Guard Protective Services is a Houston-based security firm dedicated to providing elite, discreet, and reliable protection for individuals and organizations.

Founded on the principles of integrity and tactical precision, our team comprises experienced professionals from military, law enforcement, and private security backgrounds. We understand that every client is unique, and we tailor our strategies to meet the specific threat landscape of each assignment.

We are fully licensed by the Texas Department of Public Safety (License B31226001) and carry comprehensive liability insurance. Our commitment is to your safety, your privacy, and your peace of mind.
